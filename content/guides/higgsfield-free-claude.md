<h2 id="english">English</h2>

You commented on the Higgsfield video, so here's everything: the steps, the exact connector
URL, what it really costs, and the prompts I used.

**The short version:** Higgsfield has a connector for Claude. With Higgsfield's 3-day free
trial, Claude can plan your shots, write the prompts, and generate the video, and my test cost
0 credits. After 3 days the trial becomes a paid plan unless you cancel.

### What you need

- **Claude:** a paid plan (Pro or higher). Custom connectors aren't on the free plan.
- **Higgsfield:** an account with the 3-day free trial from [higgsfield.ai/mcp](https://higgsfield.ai/mcp).
- **Time:** about one minute.

### The steps

1. Go to **[higgsfield.ai/mcp](https://higgsfield.ai/mcp)**, open the **Claude** tab, click
   **Connect MCP** and start the 3-day free trial.
2. In Claude, open **Settings → Connectors** (on the newer app it's under **Plugins → Add**)
   and choose **Add custom connector**.
3. Fill in:
   - Name: `Higgsfield`
   - URL: `https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp`
4. Click **Continue** and sign in to Higgsfield.
5. Start a new chat. Pick **Fable 5.2** (included in Pro until 16 November, 11:59 PM PT) or
   **Opus 5.5**. The connector works the same with either.
6. Paste one of the prompts below.
7. When Claude asks how to pay, choose **Use free unlimited**.
8. Set a reminder to **cancel the trial before 72 hours** if you don't want to pay.

### What "0 credits" looked like

I asked: *"using higgsfield mcp create a video of a cat flying a plane, and show how much it
cost"*. Before generating, Claude stopped and asked:

> The cat-pilot video (Seedance 2.5, 720p) costs 35 credits, but you only have 10. Your Ultra
> plan has free unlimited generations that cover this model until 17 Nov. How should I pay
> for it?
>
> **1. Use free unlimited:** Costs 0 credits; your 10 credits stay untouched.
> **2. Pay with credits:** Needs 35 credits; you have 10.

I picked option 1. A few minutes later I had a 5-second Seedance 2.5 clip of an orange cat in
aviator goggles flying a propeller plane above the clouds. That's the clip in the video.

### Prompt 1: one clip

```text
Using the Higgsfield connector, make a 5-second vertical video of a cat flying a small
propeller plane above golden clouds. Use Seedance. Before you generate, tell me the model,
the credit cost, and whether my free unlimited plan covers it.
```

### Prompt 2: a short with planned shots

```text
I want a 20-second vertical video about [your idea]. Plan 4 shots (what we see, camera move,
length). Write one video prompt per shot. Then generate each shot with the Higgsfield
connector, using the free unlimited option if it covers the model. Show me the cost before
each generation.
```

### Prompt 3: a channel plan you keep after the trial

```text
Help me start a faceless channel about [topic]. Give me: the channel idea and audience,
30 video ideas, a script template, a video prompt template for each shot, and a weekly
posting schedule. Format it as a checklist I can follow without you.
```

Run prompt 3 while the trial is on. The plan stays useful after the trial ends.

### Good to know

- **It's a trial, not a free tier.** Higgsfield says "3-day free trial". After that it becomes
  a paid plan unless you cancel.
- **Claude Pro is paid too.** The connector needs a paid Claude plan.
- **Ask for the cost first.** Prompts 1 and 2 make Claude show the credit cost before it
  generates, so nothing surprises you.
- **Models change.** The page lists Seedance 2.5 and Nano Banana Pro "and more". My run used
  Seedance 2.5.

### شكل الـ «0 كريديت»

كتبتله: *"using higgsfield mcp create a video of a cat flying a plane, and show how much it
cost"*. قبل ما يعمل الفيديو، Claude وقف وسألني: الفيديو بـ Seedance 2.5 بيكلّف 35 كريديت
وأنا معايا 10 بس، بس اشتراك Ultra فيه free unlimited بيغطي الموديل ده لحد 17 نوفمبر. واداني
اختيارين:

> **1. Use free unlimited:** بيكلّف 0 كريديت، والـ 10 كريديت بتوعك زي ما هم.
> **2. Pay with credits:** محتاج 35 كريديت، وإنت معاك 10.

اخترت الأول، وبعد دقايق كان عندي فيديو 5 ثواني بـ Seedance 2.5 لقطة برتقاني لابسة نضارة طيار
وبتسوق طيارة فوق السحاب. ده الفيديو اللي في الريل.

```text
دهبي. استخدم Seedance. قبل ما تعمله قولي الموديل وتكلفته بالكريديت، وهل الـ free unlimited
بيغطيه.
```

### برومبت 2: فيديو بلقطات متخططة

```text
اكتب برومبت فيديو لكل لقطة. وبعدين اعمل كل لقطة بـ Higgsfield connector، واستخدم الـ free
unlimited لو بيغطي الموديل. قولي التكلفة قبل كل لقطة.
```

### برومبت 3: خطة قناة تفضل معاك بعد الفري

```text

قالب سكريبت، قالب برومبت لكل لقطة، وجدول نشر أسبوعي. اكتبها checklist أقدر أمشي عليها من
```

شغّل برومبت 3 وإنت لسه في الفري، والخطة تفضل معاك بعد ما يخلص.
