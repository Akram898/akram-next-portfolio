## Seedance: direct with references

Use Seedance when you want to guide a clip with a mixture of text, images, video, and audio references.

- **Open it:** [Dreamina Seedance](https://dreamina.capcut.com/tools/seedance-2-0)
- **Best for:** character, wardrobe, motion, and style consistency across a short sequence
- **Access details:** [Seedance free-trial information](https://dreamina.capcut.com/tools/seedance-2-0)
- **Official model overview:** [Seedance 2.0 launch](https://seed.bytedance.com/blog/seedance-2-0-official-launch)

Starter prompt:

> Keep the subject identity and wardrobe consistent. Begin on a tight portrait, orbit left through the rain, then reveal the full dance pose. Natural body motion, cinematic lighting, no cuts, no text.

## Google Flow: build a connected world

Use Flow when one character or location needs to remain recognizable across several shots.

- **Open it:** [Google Flow](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow)
- **Best for:** scenes, recurring characters, storyboards, and sequence building

## Runway: control the camera

Use Runway when the source image is already strong and you mainly need a polished commercial camera move.

- **Open it:** [Runway](https://app.runwayml.com/)

## The test that saves credits

Do not send five unrelated ideas into five tools.

1. Choose one strong 4:5 image.
2. Write one six-second motion idea.
3. Generate three variations in the tool that matches the job.
4. Keep the most stable result.
5. Add names, captions, logos, and CTA text later in your editor.

Always ask the video model to preserve subject identity, product shape, composition, and color. Add only one camera move and one environmental motion per test.
