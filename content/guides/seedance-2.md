You commented **SEEDANCE20**, so here is the full version: how to try it without paying, what it actually does that the others don't, and the parts the hype posts skip.

Start with the claim doing the rounds.

## "Free and unlimited" is not true

There is **no free-forever, unlimited tier** for Seedance 2.0 on any official platform. Every route is credit-metered. Anyone telling you otherwise is either describing a trial or selling something.

What *is* true is that you can get real output without handing over a card.

- **Open it:** [Dreamina Seedance 2.0](https://dreamina.capcut.com/tools/seedance-2-0) — ByteDance's own consumer app, and the shortest path to a first clip
- **Official model post:** [Seedance 2.0 launch](https://seed.bytedance.com/blog/seedance-2-0-official-launch)

Dreamina gives logged-in users a credit allowance you can spend on video without a subscription. **Exact amounts, eligibility and region availability change often** — check the access page before planning a batch, and don't build a workflow that assumes today's number holds next month.

The honest framing: it is enough to **test the model properly before you spend anything**. It is not enough to produce a channel's worth of content.

## What actually makes it different

This is the part worth your attention, and it isn't "better quality" — everything is good now. It's the input side.

### 1. Twelve files in one generation

Seedance 2.0 accepts up to **12 reference files in a single generation** — images, video, audio and text together, not one at a time. You are not writing a prompt and hoping. You hand it a character, a location, a motion reference and a style, and it composes from them.

Its own **Precise Action Replication** demo shows this plainly: a generated result on one side, three image references and a motion reference video on the other, with the output following the reference's movement while keeping the referenced character and set.

### 2. It's the only one taking an audio reference

Of the four models people actually compare — Seedance 2.0, Veo 3.1, Sora 2, Kling 3.0 — **Seedance is the only one that accepts an audio reference** and syncs motion to it. If you are cutting to a beat, that is a genuine capability gap, not a spec-sheet nicety.

### 3. Multi-shot consistency is native

It generates **narrative sequences with multiple cohesive shots**, holding the main subject, visual style and atmosphere across shot transitions. A character can carry from one set to a completely different one without you re-rolling them and hoping the face matches.

## The 15-second cap, and how to get past it

Worth knowing before you plan anything long: **a single generation maxes out at 15 seconds.** The valid durations are preset — 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15 — not any number you like.

You get past it with **Extend**: take the last frame of a clip and continue from it, repeatedly. Chained that way there is no practical ceiling, and continuity holds because each segment starts from the previous one's final frame.

So "minutes of continuous footage" is true — as a **chain of ≤15s generations**, not one long render. Plan your shots around that and it's a non-issue. Assume you can ask for 90 seconds in one go and you'll be annoyed.

## Where the others still win

Nothing here is the best at everything, and anyone claiming a clean winner hasn't used all four.

| If you need | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Multimodal control, references, story consistency | **Seedance 2.0** | 12-file input, audio reference, native multi-shot |
| Cinema-grade output and native audio | **Veo 3.1** | Highest raw quality ceiling |
| Physics realism and longer single clips | **Sora 2** | Best simulation, longest single generation |
| Cheapest per second, generous free tier | **Kling 3.0** | Best value if budget is the constraint |

Pick on the constraint that actually binds you. If your problem is "the character changes between shots", Seedance solves it. If your problem is "I need one 25-second unbroken take", it doesn't.

## Getting your first clip out

1. Open [Dreamina](https://dreamina.capcut.com/tools/seedance-2-0) and sign in. No card needed to start.
2. Check your current credit balance **before** generating — it tells you how many attempts you actually have.
3. Start at a **short duration**. A 4–6s test costs a fraction of a 15s one and tells you the same thing about whether your prompt and references are working.
4. Add references rather than lengthening the prompt. One clear character image beats three paragraphs describing them.
5. Only once a look is landing, spend credits on the longer version.
6. Need it longer? Generate, then **Extend** from the last frame.

The mistake that burns a free allowance fastest is generating at max duration while still figuring out the prompt.

## Before you commit money

- **Terms move.** Free credits, regional access and per-second pricing on this category change month to month. Re-check the pricing page the day you plan a paid batch.
- **Version names are confusing.** You'll see Seedance 1.0, 2.0 and 2.5 referenced across ByteDance's model pages, Dreamina's app and third-party resellers, sometimes on the same day. Confirm which version a given page is actually selling you.
- **Third-party resellers** wrap the model at their own prices and their own limits. Going through the official app first tells you what the model actually does, before a reseller's quota shapes your opinion of it.
